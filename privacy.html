Privacy Policy

Aura Medical · Edict Digital · Last updated: October 16, 2026

Summary: Aura Medical does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your study progress stays on your device.

Data we collect

None. The app works entirely offline. We do not operate servers that receive data from the app and we do not include third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

Data stored on your device

Your study progress (reviewed cards, review schedule, bookmarks, quiz results, streak) and your preferences (daily goal, reminder time, theme) are stored locally on your device. They may be included in your device's own backups (iCloud on iPhone and iPad, Google backup on Android) according to your device settings; those backups are handled by Apple or Google, not by us. You can erase them at any time in Settings → Reset all progress or by deleting the app.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) on iPhone and iPad, and by Google (Google Play) on Android. We do not receive your payment details. The app only asks the store whether your account has an active Aura Pro entitlement. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your App Store account settings or in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions.

Notifications

If you turn on daily reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. No data is sent to us or to a push notification service.

Children

Aura Medical is an educational app intended for students and adults. We do not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Medical disclaimer

Aura Medical is an educational tool and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
